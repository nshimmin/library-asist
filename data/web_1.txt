Your Next Adventure Begins Here: Iowa Adventure Pass provides library cardholders the opportunity to discover and explore participating museums, gardens, zoos, and other destinations across the state.

## How it works:

- North Liberty residents ages 18 years of age and older, with a valid library card may reserve passes.
- At the destination you must present a valid photo ID and printed pass or the physical pass for the Quad Cities Fun Bundle (email on phone not accepted)
- Passes allow entrance for two adults and two children unless otherwise noted. Additional tickets can be purchased at the venue.
- Iowa Adventure Pass cannot be used for special events (e.g. Zoo Brew or Night Eyes)
- If plans change please cancel your reservation by calling the North Liberty Library at (319)626-5701

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Iowa Libraries Adventure Pass Program?**

Adventure Pass is an online program that allows library patrons to use their library cards to visit area attractions, museums, and parks for free.

**How often can I reserve the pass?**

You can reserve one pass per venue every 365 days.

**Can I show the confirmation email on my mobile device?**

No. You MUST print your reservation confirmation and show the printed, physical copy at the door to enter. If you do not have a printer, you can print your pass at the library.

### Our Destinations

**The African American Museum of Iowa**, located in Cedar Rapids, is the only statewide museum devoted to preserving African American history and culture.

**Blank Park Zoo** in Des Moines aims to inspire an appreciation of the natural world through conservation, education, research, and recreation.

**The Greater Des Moines Botanical Garden** in Des Moines cultivates diverse plants and connects people to the environment surrounding them.

**Grout Museum District**, located in Waterloo, truly has something for everyone… from Victoriana to Veterans and Science to Stars.

**The History Center**, located in the historic Douglas Mansion, showcases a number of unique and interesting artifacts, photos, stories and information regarding the history of Linn County’s culture, people, businesses, neighborhoods and more.

**The Science Center of Iowa** in downtown Des Moines features hands-on exhibits, special-format theaters and unique programming.

**Niabi Zoo & Putnam Museum** passes are available in the library on a first come basis for a three-day loan period. These memberships are no longer managed through the Iowa Adventure Pass reservation software and you must check out the physical membership pass in the library.

### Museum for All

Through Museums for All, those receiving food assistance (SNAP benefits) can gain free or reduced admission to more than 1,000 museums throughout the United States simply by presenting their EBT card and a photo ID.
