The Book Bike brings the library to the community from May – October! When you spot the Book Bike stop by to check out books, sign up for a library card, and learn more about library services and programs!

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I pick up holds and ILLs at the Book Bike?**

If you would like to pick-up holds at the Book Bike please contact the library to make arrangements.

**How do I get a library card?**

You can register for a library card at the Book Bike or online.

**How do I return books?**

You can return your books to the Book Bike or the North Liberty Library.

**Where do I find schedule updates?**

In the event of inclement weather a Book Bike stop may be canceled. Visit our daily calendar for the most up-to-date information.

### Bike Friendly Designation

The North Liberty Community Center was designated bike-friendly in 2022 by Bike Iowa City, a local organization supporting local biking and bike tourism. This designation acknowledges the amenities and offerings the Community Center has for cyclists.

- Information on local tails
- Convenient bike storage
- Bike Repair Kits at Recreation and Library front desks with patch kits and inner tubes
- Bike locks available for checkout in the library.
- Helmets available for purchase in the Recreation Department for $12 each
- Bike registration offered through the Police Department, which can help us get your bike back to you if it’s lost or stolen
