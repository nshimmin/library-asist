## Q: Can I get something notarized at the library?
Also asked as notary, notary public, notarize, notarization
A: Yes with a couple exceptions.  Library staff are not able to notarize subpoenas or items regarding wills or estates. Notarization service availability is based on staff availability, so it is recommended that persons call the library first to make sure one is available. 

## Q: Can I renew my driver's license at the library?
Also asked as driver's license, drivers license kiosk, license renewal, DOT kiosk, renew license, ID renewal
A: No. The library had a driver's license renewal kiosk many years ago, but it is no longer here. Everything that was handled by the kiosk is now handled online through the Iowa Department of Transportation's website https://iowadot.gov/drivers-licenses-ids/get-or-renew-drivers-licenses-ids-permits/renew-drivers-license or by making an appointment at the Coralville DMV.
