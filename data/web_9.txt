## Help Set Our Direction

Volunteers, appointed by North Liberty’s Mayor and confirmed by the City Council, help advise on a wide range of areas, including land use and zoning, parks and recreation programs, and library policy.

Appointment to a volunteer board or commission means you can make a difference in many aspects of community development. Take this opportunity to volunteer your time to serve on any one of the following. Most terms begin on July 1 of each year, but openings sometimes occur during the year.

## Board & Commission Meetings

Board and commission meetings are open to the public and typically meet on a recurring cycle in the City Council Chambers at City HAll, 360 N. Main St. You can attend these meetings live in person, or watch them live online.

## Apply to Serve

Applications are available at City Hall, 360 N. Main Street or online. Completed applications can be submitted at City Hall or via email to \[email protected\]. Applicants must be 18 years old and a legal resident of the city.

Appointments are made by the Mayor with the City Council’s approval. Commissions that assist in department overview and making recommendations to City Council. Members are appointed by the mayor and approved by the city council.
