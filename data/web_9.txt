## Help Set Our Direction

Volunteers, appointed by North Liberty’s Mayor and confirmed by the City
Council, help advise on a wide range of areas, including land use and zoning,
parks and recreation programs, and library policy.

Appointment to a volunteer board or commission means you can make a difference
in many aspects of community development. Take this opportunity to volunteer
your time to serve on any one of the following. Most terms begin on July 1 of
each year, but openings sometimes occur during the year.

## Board & Commission Meetings

Board and commission meetings are open to the public and typically meet on a
recurring cycle in the City Council Chambers at City HAll, 360 N. Main St. You
can attend these meetings live in person, or watch them live online.

[ Agendas, Minutes & Videos ](https://northlibertyiowa.portal.civicclerk.com/)
