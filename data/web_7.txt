The North Liberty Library’s meeting and study spaces are available during operating hours on a first-come, first-served basis for individual, nonprofit, educational, civic, and informational gatherings that are open to the public. Use of the rooms does not imply endorsement by the Library or the City of North Liberty, and commercial sales and private parties are not permitted.

## Reservation Details

Choose your date, start time, end time (up to three hours), and group size, then select Search to view available spaces. Once you’ve selected a room, you’ll be prompted to enter your reservation details, including your name, phone number, and email.

- An individual or group may have up to three (3) reservations scheduled at a time.
- Meeting/study rooms may be reserved for a maximum of three (3) hours unless prearranged; extensions may be permitted upon request if space is available.
