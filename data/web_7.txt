# Room Reservations

![](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-
content/uploads/2026/04/Header_Shape_Bottom-1.svg)

The North Liberty Library’s meeting and study spaces are available during
operating hours on a first-come, first-served basis for individual, nonprofit,
educational, civic, and informational gatherings that are open to the public.
Use of the rooms does not imply endorsement by the Library or the City of
North Liberty, and commercial sales and private parties are not permitted.

## Reservation Details

  * An individual or group may have up to four (4) reservations scheduled at a time.
  * Meeting/study rooms may be reserved for a maximum of three (3) hours unless prearranged; extensions may be permitted upon request if space is available.
