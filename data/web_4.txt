# Reading Challenges

![](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-
content/uploads/2026/04/Header_Shape_Bottom-1.svg)

## Winter Reading Challenge

Bundle up with great reads! Our Winter Reading Challenge invites readers of
all ages to stay cozy, discover new stories, and track their reading each
winter. Join the fun, earn small rewards, and keep the joy of reading glowing
through the coldest months.

## Summer Reading Program

Dive into a summer packed with stories, activities, and community fun! Our
Summer Reading Program invites readers of all ages to explore new worlds, try
exciting challenges, and celebrate the joy of reading. Track your progress,
earn cool prizes, and join us for special events all summer long—from hands-on
crafts and energetic performances to family activities and teen hangouts.

## 1000 Books Before Kindergarten

1,000 Books Before Kindergarten is a fun, self-paced early literacy program
designed to build a love of reading from the very beginning. Every book counts
(yes, repeats too!).
