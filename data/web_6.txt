## Discover More with your Library Card

Dive into a world of learning, entertainment, and exploration—available anytime, anywhere with your library card! From streaming movies and listening to audiobooks to mastering new skills, researching trusted sources, and learning languages, our digital resources are here to spark curiosity and support your goals. Browse the full collection below and start exploring all the incredible tools your library offers—free, fun, and just a click away!

## Consumer Reports

Compare price, performance, and reliability all in one place with expert reviews.

## Driving Tests

Download the official Iowa DOT handbook and then use these practice tests and resources to get familiar with everything you will be asked on permit or DL tests for car, motorcycle, or commercial vehicle drivers.

## Glassdoor

Glassdoor helps people everywhere find jobs and companies they love. Search all the open positions on the web. Read reviews on over 600,000 companies worldwide.

## Kanopy

Kanopy is an on-demand streaming video platform available to residents of North Liberty and rural Johnson County. Watch over 30000 Documentaries, Classic and Indie Films via Desktop, Mobile and Roku. *\*\*Please NOTE: Access up to 15 titles per month. Once a title has been selected – that counts as 1 title and is available for 72 hours.*

## Libby

Libby is a free app where you can borrow ebooks, digital audiobooks, and magazines from the library. You can stream titles with Wi-Fi or mobile data, or download them for offline use and read anytime, anywhere.

## LingQ

LingQ turns real content into interactive language lessons. Patrons can learn through TV shows, podcasts, music, or articles they already enjoy, saving new words directly inside the sentences where they appear and building a personal vocabulary library over time.

## Mango

Mango Languages provides a self-paced learning program that teaches languages through relevant and topical dialogue using the four key components — vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar, and culture — for improving proficiency in another language.

## Momentrix

Mometrix eLibrary provides a wide range of study materials to help test-takers, including flashcards, practice tests, and guides, covering subjects from foundational education to specialized trades and advanced academic fields.

## Niche Academy

Niche Academy is a simple learning platform with easy to follow tutorials. Learn how to use anything from social media platforms to resources available from the North Liberty Library.

## North Liberty Digital History Collection

Search through North Liberty Historical records. Letters, photos, books, and more! Access is free and we invite you to contribute to the collection!

## ParentTV

ParentTV offers thousands of on-demand parenting videos and courses with content designed to support those who care for children, from pregnancy through to teenage years.

## The Gazette

While inside the North Liberty Community Center enjoy unlimited free access to thegazette.com. Made possible in partnership with The Gazette and your area public libraries.

## The New York Times

Enjoy free access to NYTimes.com courtesy of your libraries in Digital Johnson County. To activate your subscription, you’ll need to log in or register (with your email and password). Please note, you will need to re-redeem access every 72 hours.

## The Wall Street Journal

Get unlimited access to breaking business, financial and world news from The Wall Street Journal. To activate your subscription, you’ll need to log in or register (with your email and password). Please note, you will need to re-redeem access every 72 hours.

## The Washington Post

The Washington Post provides world-class coverage of the day’s most important local, national and international news.

## Unite for Literacy

Unite for Literacy provides engaging digital picture books complete with audio narration options in a variety of languages.

## Value Line

Value Line provides access to leading research publications covering stocks, mutual funds, options and convertible securities as well as our proprietary special situation stocks.

## World Book Online

A comprehensive suite of e-learning resources designed for all ages and abilities with articles, videos, educator tools, eBooks, research guides and more.
