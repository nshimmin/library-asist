## Discover More with your Library Card

Dive into a world of learning, entertainment, and exploration—available
anytime, anywhere with your library card! From streaming movies and listening
to audiobooks to mastering new skills, researching trusted sources, and
learning languages, our digital resources are here to spark curiosity and
support your goals. Browse the full collection below and start exploring all
the incredible tools your library offers—free, fun, and just a click away!

## Driving Tests

Download the official Iowa DOT handbook and then use these practice tests and
resources to get familiar with everything you will be asked on permit or DL
tests for car, motorcycle, or commercial vehicle drivers.

[ Visit ](https://driving-tests.org/iowa/)

Glassdoor helps people everywhere find jobs and companies they love. Search

worldwide.

[ Visit ](https://www.glassdoor.com/index.htm)

## Kanopy

Kanopy is an on-demand streaming video platform available to residents of
North Liberty and rural Johnson County. Watch over 30000 Documentaries,
Classic and Indie Films via Desktop, Mobile and Roku. _**Please NOTE: Access
up to 15 titles per month. Once a title has been selected – that counts as 1
title and is available for 72 hours._

[ Visit ](https://www.kanopy.com/en/northlibertylibrary)

![""](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Libby-
logo.webp)

## Libby

Libby is a free app where you can borrow ebooks, digital audiobooks, and
magazines from the library. You can stream titles with Wi-Fi or mobile data,
or download them for offline use and read anytime, anywhere.

[ Visit ](https://icpl.overdrive.com/)

![""](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-
content/uploads/2026/05/Group-179939835-1-1.webp)

## LingQ

LingQ turns real content into interactive language lessons. Patrons can learn
through TV shows, podcasts, music, or articles they already enjoy, saving new
words directly inside the sentences where they appear and building a personal
vocabulary library over time.

[ Visit ](https://www.lingq.com/settings/library-group)

![""](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/mango-
languages-logo-2-crop-3.webp)

## Mango

Mango Languages provides a self-paced learning program that teaches languages
through relevant and topical dialogue using the four key components —
vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar, and culture — for improving proficiency in
another language.

[ Visit
](https://learn.mangolanguages.com/login?gymToken=63rSpYKjQJJfCPbc8EMygg&r=https%3A%2F%2Fnorthlibertylibrary.org%2Freference%2F)

## Momentrix

Mometrix eLibrary provides a wide range of study materials to help test-
takers, including flashcards, practice tests, and guides, covering subjects
from foundational education to specialized trades and advanced academic
fields.

[ Visit ](https://portal.mometrixelibrary.com/iowa-gateway?p=%2F&__acct=17197)

## The New York Times

Enjoy free access to NYTimes.com courtesy of your libraries in Digital Johnson
County. To activate your subscription, you’ll need to log in or register (with

72 hours.
