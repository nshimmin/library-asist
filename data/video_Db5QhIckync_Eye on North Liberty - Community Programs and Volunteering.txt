Title: Eye on North Liberty - Community Programs and Volunteering
Published: 2026-08-04 18:05:09
Video ID: Db5QhIckync

In North Liberty, volunteering isn't complicated. It's just neighbors showing up for neighbors, and there's a place for everyone. Serve up summer with summer lunch and fun, a program bringing free meals to kids at the ranch house all summer long. Volunteers help with setup and serving. No experience required. Just show up ready to help. You'll need to be at least 12 years old to sign up, and shifts are open to individuals and groups alike. Love a good party? North Liberty's Blues and Barbecue needs nearly 250 volunteers to pull off the town's biggest summer festival. Help out in the beverage garden, with inflatables, or wherever you're needed. Shifts run 2 to 4 hours, and volunteers as young as 14 can sign up on their own. You'll walk away with a free t-shirt, a snack voucher, and a pretty great story. Prefer something quieter? The North Liberty Library welcomes volunteers 18 and up with a youth program for students in 5th through 12th grade who want to get involved, too. Whatever your interest, there's likely a role that fits it. Curious how your city really works? The neighborhood ambassador program takes a small group of residents through a series of sessions each year, connecting them with city staff and giving them the tools and even grant funding to build stronger neighborhoods. It's a real behind-the-scenes look at how North Liberty runs and a chance to turn what you learn into something lasting for your block. Ready for something bigger? The North Liberty Fire Department is always looking for volunteer firefighters and EMS responders. You'll need to be 18 with a valid driver's license. Full training is provided from firefighter certification to EMT. So, all you really need to bring is the willingness. stock shelves, tend the garden, deliver groceries. The community pantry relies on volunteers of nearly every age to help feed and clothe our neighbors with flexible hours to fit any schedule from a couple hours a week to a couple hours a month. Even younger volunteers can get involved through the garden or help alongside a parent. And this is just the start. From schools to nonprofits across town, North Liberty needs you however you can give.
