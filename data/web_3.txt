Our StoryWalk® brings books to life, page by page, along an outdoor path the whole family can enjoy. As you stroll, you’ll read a featured picture book displayed in sequence—perfect for wiggly little readers, fresh-air seekers, and anyone who loves stories with a side of adventure. It’s a playful blend of literacy, movement and community fun, and it’s available anytime you’re ready for a walk and a good tale.

**Presented in partnership with Veridian Credit Union**

## Listen to your librarian read the story as you stroll along

North Liberty’s permanent StoryWalk®, located at Liberty Centre, 795 W Cherry St., and is updated regularly by the librarians at the North Liberty Library. It was made possible by Veridian Credit Union.

The StoryWalk® concept was created by Anne Ferguson of Montpelier, VT and was developed with the help of Rachel Senechal, Kellogg-Hubbard Library.
