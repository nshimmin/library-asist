# Storywalk

![](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-
content/uploads/2026/04/Header_Shape_Bottom-1.svg)

![A book on an interactive screen.](https://northlibertylibrary.org/wp-
content/uploads/2026/05/StoryWalk-Spring-2025-1.webp)

Our StoryWalk® brings books to life, page by page, along an outdoor path the
whole family can enjoy. As you stroll, you’ll read a featured picture book
displayed in sequence—perfect for wiggly little readers, fresh-air seekers,
and anyone who loves stories with a side of adventure. It’s a playful blend of
literacy, movement and community fun, and it’s available anytime you’re ready
for a walk and a good tale.

**Presented in partnership with Veridian Credit Union**

## Listen to your librarian read the story as you stroll along

[North Liberty Library](https://soundcloud.com/nlibertylibrary "North Liberty
Library") · [Story Walks](https://soundcloud.com/nlibertylibrary/sets/story-
walks "Story Walks")

North Liberty’s permanent StoryWalk®, located at Liberty Centre, 795 W Cherry
St., and is updated regularly by the librarians at the North Liberty Library.
It was made possible by Veridian Credit Union.

The StoryWalk® concept was created by Anne Ferguson of Montpelier, VT and was
developed with the help of Rachel Senechal, Kellogg-Hubbard Library.
